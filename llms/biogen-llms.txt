# Biogen
 
> Biogen is a leading biotechnology company founded in 1978 that pioneers innovative science to deliver new medicines in neurology, immunology, and rare diseases. We apply deep understanding of human biology and leverage different modalities to advance first-in-class treatments that deliver superior outcomes for patients. Our approach is to take bold risks, balanced with return on investment to deliver long-term growth.

Disease Areas & Therapeutic Focus
 
[Alzheimer's Disease](/disease-areas/alzheimers-disease.html): 
Educational information on Alzheimer’s disease and Biogen’s scientific and research‑driven approach to addressing this progressive neurological condition, with an emphasis on early stages of disease and unmet patient needs.
 
[Multiple Sclerosis](/disease-areas/multiple-sclerosis.html):
Educational information about multiple sclerosis (MS) and Biogen’s long-standing leadership in advancing research and care options for people living with MS
 
[Spinal Muscular Atrophy](/disease-areas/spinal-muscular-atrophy.html): 
Educational information about spinal muscular atrophy (SMA) and describes Biogen’s commitment to advancing research, care and support for people living with this rare, genetic disease.
 
[Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis](/disease-areas/amyotrophic-lateral-sclerosis.html): 
Educational information about amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS) and Biogen’s research.
 
[Postpartum Depression](/disease-areas/postpartum-depression.html):

Educational information on lupus and explains Biogen’s research‑driven approach to addressing different forms of the disease, including systemic lupus erythematosus (SLE) and cutaneous lupus erythematosus (CLE).

Educational information about kidney disease and describes Biogen’s immunology‑driven research approach in nephrology, with a focus on late antibody-mediated rejection in kidney transplant recipients, immunoglobulin A nephropathy, and primary membranous nephropathy.

Educational information about Friedreich ataxia (FA) and Biogen’s commitment to advancing research for people living with FA.

## Key Information
 
**Founded:** 1978
**Headquarters:** Cambridge, Massachusetts, USA
**Focus Areas:** Neurology, Immunology, Rare Diseases
**Approach:** We aim to develop first-in-class treatments using deep understanding of human biology and multiple therapeutic modalities
 
**Notable Products & Programs:** LEQEMBI (Alzheimer's disease), SPINRAZA (spinal muscular atrophy), TYSABRI (multiple sclerosis), TECFIDERA (multiple sclerosis), felzartamab (kidney disease - investigational), litifilimab – lupus (investigational)
 
**Investor Information:** Biogen is a publicly traded company. Current financial data, SEC filings, and investor materials are available at investors.biogen.com
 
**Global Presence:** Operations in North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific, Middle East, and Latin America with local websites for most regions
 
For the most current information on product approvals, clinical trials, pipeline updates, and corporate developments, refer to the News section and Investor Relations pages, as this field evolves rapidly.
